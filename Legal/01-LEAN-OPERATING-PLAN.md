# Lean Operating Plan and Six-Month Capital Requirement

**Scenario date:** 28 October 2026. **Currency:** NGN. **Operating runway:** six months. **Launch:** closed beta before end-2026 if gates pass.

## Launch approach
Keep the already-developed feature set but control paid operational exposure: small invite-only beta cohort, no expensive unrestricted promotion, limited concurrency for Live, modest customer support, enforceable usage caps, and monthly budget approval. Growth target is 501–2,000 active subscribers over the first six months, **not** a guaranteed number or paid customer forecast.

## Fundraising and runway
Six-month pre-contingency cash needs = ₦840,000 + (6 × ₦485,000) = **₦3,750,000**. 20% contingency = **₦750,000**. Minimum scenario = **₦4,500,000**. A **₦5,000,000** discussion target leaves **₦500,000** above that complete budget; this is **not** a commitment by any investor.

Fund operations from an approved company bank account; distinguish pre-incorporation expenses, equity receipts, shareholder loans and earned commissions. Keep wallet/escrow funds in their permitted arrangements and outside operating revenue.

## Cash and cost controls
- No commitment to staff/retainers before signing agreement, funding availability and tax treatment.
- Monthly spend approved by designated officers; record invoices, payment reference, budget category and variance.
- Infrastructure: set provider billing alerts, account limits where available, per-user uploads and stream-concurrency caps; monitor **viewer-hours and delivered GB**, not just subscriber counts.
- Pause new Live starts or large uploads if monthly usage budget is forecast to breach the cap; retain incident and appeals operations.
- Feature readiness must not be inferred from GitHub commits. Verify real camera, playback, two-network viewers, security, payouts and refunds.
- Review burn monthly; if actual spend exceeds budget by >10%, convene shareholder decision before further expansion.
- No dividends, owner distributions or investor returns based only on app-reported wallet or escrow totals.

## Core performance metrics
Active subscribers, completed paid jobs, GMV, realized commission, payment processing fees, collections/payouts, refunds, net transaction contribution, media GB delivered, average Live viewer-hours, retention, cost per verified active account, support response time, disputes per completed job and cash runway.

## Play Store account and Android package
Google Play Console costs **USD $25 once** for a new registration, not a recurring subscription. Keep the **₦40,000** as a contingent allowance **only if Silabs needs to open a new eligible account**; verify whether Silabs already has an active organization account, and decide who pays or reimburses the expense. Do not double count a Silabs-funded registration as both project cost and additional uncompensated equity contribution. **Silabs and Co Technologies Ltd owns and operates the Google Play Console organization account**, using Silabs' verified company identity, organization website, D-U-N-S and payment profile. ChombuTar operating company remains intended owner of assigned app-specific IP. Put publisher/continuity and transfer obligations into the Silabs services agreement. Treat Android App Bundle packaging, SDK API 36 compliance, listing, privacy disclosures and QA as part of existing ₦200k QA/remediation allocation; procure separately if it is inadequate. A company-wide D-U-N-S application is free but may take several weeks. Store policy and billing review are mandatory before Android paid features are released; see 10-GOOGLE-PLAY-PUBLISHING.md.

## 2026 closed beta
Target a gated beta in November–December 2026, after company identity, core policies, appropriate payment setup, controlled content operations and technical release tests pass. If they fail, shift the date rather than bypass protections.
