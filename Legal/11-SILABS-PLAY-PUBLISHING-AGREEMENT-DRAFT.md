# DRAFT — Google Play Publishing and Account Custody Agreement (Term Sheet)

**Parties:** (1) Silabs and Co Technologies Ltd (the **Developer Account Holder / Publisher**); (2) [the newly incorporated ChombuTar operating company], registered particulars to be inserted (the **App Business Owner**).

**Status:** Negotiation-only drafting instructions, not executed or binding. The Google developer account has **not been confirmed existing, registered, verified or paid**. Nigeria-qualified counsel should verify legal and taxation obligations and prepare the binding arrangement.

## 1. Defined roles and ownership
1. Silabs legally owns and controls the Google Play Console **organization developer account** and acts as publisher of the ChombuTar Android app under its own verified organization identity.
2. ChombuTar operating company remains the intended owner of all expressly assigned ChombuTar-specific software, designs, branding, app-facing content rights, and contractual customer relationships, subject to third-party and end-user rights. **Account ownership does not equal copyright or trademark ownership.**
3. ChombuTar gives Silabs a limited licence as needed to compile, upload, maintain, advertise and distribute the approved Android app through Silabs' Play Console solely for the agreed project and term.
4. No blanket right to exploit app code/brand for other clients; no automatic entitlement to wallet or booking revenue solely because Silabs holds the developer account.

## 2. Registration and cost
- Verify whether Silabs already has a compliant, active **organization** Play account; if not, register with Silabs' CAC organization details, D-U-N-S, verified legal address, official website, authorized representative and correct Google payments profile.
- New Google Play Console registration costs US$25 **once**. A ₦40,000 contingency is in the existing lean launch budget **only if** a new account is necessary.
- Registration expense payer: **[Silabs / ChombuTar / reimbursed — undecided]**. Any reimbursed spending is not also credited as an unpaid equity contribution.
- Recording of Google service fees, tax, and refunds: **[Agree payer, accounting and reporting]**.

## 3. Administrative and publication controls
- Silabs retains secure primary account-owner credentials and MFA; no shared Google password.
- ChombuTar appoints named representatives for **role-scoped access** to releases, store listing, analytics, policy notifications and financial reporting to the extent offered by Google and appropriate.
- **Approved app name/package ID, website, privacy policy, developer disclosure, content rating, support contacts and store listing** must be documented and accurate; published developer legal identity remains consistent with Silabs' actual account information.
- Silabs requires ChombuTar's designated written approval before material changes to branding, major app permissions, pricing, feature access, payment flows or production rollout, except for urgent legal/security action followed by prompt notification.
- Control register: source repository, AAB build process, app signing/export records, Play App Signing arrangements, API keys, signing key recovery, Firebase/Analytics integrations, domain verification, linked integrations and backup administrators.
- Define authority to unpublish/suspend: emergency policy/legal response permitted when necessary, with prompt notice and remediation cooperation; ordinary unilateral removal without agreed notice prohibited by contract to the extent legally permissible.
- Set reporting cadence **[weekly/monthly]**, uptime/incident alert windows **[__]**, release response targets **[__]**, audit rights and minimum records retained.

## 4. Payments, publisher liability and taxes
- **Physical services and real-world job bookings** must be distinguished from **in-app digital subscriptions, upgrades, premium placement and credits**; apply the correct Google Play Billing policies for each product.
- Google Play merchant identity and receipt destination may depend on payment product and merchant/publisher setup. If receipts are collected under Silabs' merchant/payment profile, specify whether Silabs is merchant of record or collection agent, required remittance and reconciliation timetable, processor fees, VAT/other tax reporting, refunds and chargeback liabilities. **Do not presume funds automatically belong to ChombuTar or Silabs.**
- Paystack wallet/escrow settlement and legal licensing are governed separately and must be signed off before commercial launch.

## 5. Privacy and compliance responsibility
- ChombuTar controls business purposes of customer data to the extent established by law; Silabs has obligations as publisher and technical processor/operator as appropriate.
- Define who prepares, approves and updates Play Data safety declarations, policy statements, age/content rating, account deletion, camera/microphone disclosures, moderation and abuse reports. The published declarations must match the actual SDKs and systems.
- Provide necessary confidentiality, DPA, breach notice, secure account access and user rights cooperation.

## 6. Term, termination and app transfer
- Term **[__]**, renewal **[__]**, termination for cause **[__]**, cure period **[__]**, handover window **[__]** to negotiate.
- On termination, deadlock, Silabs insolvency, change of control or the company's decision to migrate publishing: Silabs must reasonably assist ChombuTar in **Google's official app-transfer procedure** to a properly verified, active eligible developer account.
- The target account may need an active Google payments profile for paid apps/in-app products; certain integrations, monetization details and store assets can require additional work. **A transfer cannot be promised instant or unconditional**, and is subject to Google's approvals, policies and limitations.
