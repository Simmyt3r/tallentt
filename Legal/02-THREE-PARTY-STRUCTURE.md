## Corporate and intellectual property
The incorporated ChombuTar company is intended to own **specifically assigned company IP**, including agreed project source code, business assets, brand, designs and databases (subject to user ownership/privacy and existing licenses). Do not assert that a shareholding automatically transfers copyrights. Prepare documented transfer/assignment or exclusive perpetual licence as appropriate, with:
1. IP inventory of ChombuTar-specific code, generated designs and proprietary workflows.
2. Separate schedule of Silabs pre-existing tools, generalized components, third-party libraries, open-source restrictions and non-transferable rights.
3. Warranties on originality, confidentiality, permissions, encumbrances, infringement risk and IP provenance.
4. Company administrator/control rights over GitHub repositories, production infrastructure, DNS, databases, payment accounts, signing keys, cloud billing, backups and credentials.
5. Code export, handover, continuity and termination assistance, including repository access after an ownership dispute.

## Play Store publisher exception and IP separation
Silabs shall be the **legal holder/operator of the Google Play Console developer account** for ChombuTar's Android distribution, subject to eligibility and a signed app-publishing services agreement. The future ChombuTar operating company retains intended ownership of specifically assigned ChombuTar app IP, branding, customer relationship and agreed user data rights. Publisher-account control does **not** itself transfer app copyright, trademarks or marketplace revenues to Silabs. Give company-designated personnel scoped release/reporting access without sharing Silabs' primary credentials. Record who controls listing metadata, app package, app-signing material, incident response, Play Billing setup and merchant payouts. Require cooperation and official app transfer upon qualifying termination; transfer is subject to Google requirements and cannot be guaranteed unconditional. See 11-SILABS-PLAY-PUBLISHING-AGREEMENT-DRAFT.md.
