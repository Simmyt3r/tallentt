# Revenue, Market Comparison and Commission Scenarios

## Current position
No external users and **no verified earned commercial revenue**. The repository contains a Live-support-fee accrual reporting path, but do not assert that it has actually collected fees. Deal commissions and sponsorships currently lack reconciled evidence; wallet liabilities and secured escrow balances are **not platform income**.

## Revenue model
**First product:** marketplace service commissions on *successfully completed jobs*. Rate is **not approved**. Compare **8%** versus **10%**, and test small job bands before applying. Other ideas (subscriptions, advertising, premium visibility) remain unapproved and should not fund a six-month runway forecast.

Use two payment paths only after confirming the legal position: (a) protected, partner-processed collection and releases with clear fee disclosure and reconciled commission, and (b) client-to-talent direct payments, with no presumption of enforceable or collectible commission outside the platform.

## Local processor fee reference
Paystack's Nigerian local standard collection fee: **1.5% + ₦100**, capped at ₦2,000; the ₦100 is waived for transactions below ₦2,500. Transfer fees: ₦10 up to ₦5,000, ₦25 for ₦5,001–₦50,000 and ₦50 above ₦50,000. Actual commercial, VAT, chargeback and settlement terms must be checked with the selected processor. Source: https://paystack.com/pricing

## Job contribution examples
Assume completed job value **₦50,000**, one local collection (₦850), two ₦25 payouts (₦50 total), with ChombuTar bearing these fees.

| Parameter | 8% | 10% |
|---|---:|---:|
| Gross commission | ₦4,000 | ₦5,000 |
| Collection + payout fees | ₦900 | ₦900 |
| **Contribution before other variable costs, tax and fixed opex** | **₦3,100** | **₦4,100** |
| Jobs to cover ₦485,000 monthly lean fixed costs | **157** | **119** |

This example does NOT establish the true average ticket. Actual payout costs differ with amount, payment arrangement, user behavior, refund/dispute frequency and whether fees are charged to consumers or talents.

## Competitor comparison (review before setting rates)
- **Fiverr:** fixed marketplace seller-fee model; valuable benchmark for packaged offers, platform mediation and transparent transaction fees. https://www.fiverr.com/
- **Upwork:** variable fee structures, contract tools and disputes; benchmark for formal marketplace trust and longer jobs. https://www.upwork.com/
- **Trustboy:** Nigerian services-marketplace competitor to review via documented website and firsthand sign-up testing; avoid unverified pricing claims.
- Consider other Nigerian local competitors and off-platform WhatsApp referral behavior.

ChombuTar differentiation proposition: verifiable, shareable, revocable Work Pass with work history, privacy protections, disputes and local market fit. A verification badge is not professional certification.

## Measurement after beta
Completed jobs; gross merchandise value; 8% vs 10% conversion; refund/chargeback rate; processor effective rate; actual commission accrued/settled; dispute costs; cost of winning user; repeat job frequency. Confirm positive unit contribution **before** raising ad spend.

## Subscription and advertising future options
Require fresh product approval, separate policy disclosures, accurate tax classification and user-consent review. No fictional sponsor or subscription revenue in forecasts.
